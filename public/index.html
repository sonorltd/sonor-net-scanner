How to read the result · port reference

Open: something is listening, so the service is on. Refused: the device is there but nothing listens on that port (switched off in its settings, or a different model). No answer: a firewall is dropping it, the device is off, or it is on a VLAN this scanner cannot route to. UDP services (DHCP, SNMP, mDNS, SSDP, UniFi discovery) cannot be seen by a TCP check: use the Discovery tab for those.
